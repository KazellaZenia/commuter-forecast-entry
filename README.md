One line of weather forecast for the city that does not exist: Mizzlewick — light rain, heavy sighs, and the 7:52 to Nowhere Central delayed by leaves, as it has been every autumn since 1987.

`main.py` (pure stdlib) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
